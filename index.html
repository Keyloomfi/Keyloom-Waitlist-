What is the BuyReady Score?

The BuyReady Score measures how prepared you are to purchase a home, refinance, or tap into home equity based on your income, savings, credit, and overall financial profile.

Unlike a credit score, the BuyReady Score shows how close you are to becoming mortgage-ready.
